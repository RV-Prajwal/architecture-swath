### Architectural concept

The client wanted a contemporary home strongly connected to **Kerala's cultural architectural traditions**.

The main organizing principle was a **north-to-south axis**, inspired by Kerala Vastu.

That axis runs conceptually from:

**main gate → entrance → central spine → backyard**

The house is divided into:

**public → semi-private → private**

zones. [Biltrax Media, A Biltrax Group venture](https://www.biltraxmedia.com/house-neeranjanam-bengaluru-architecture-swath/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

A central courtyard connects living and dining areas with bedrooms above.

# 27. Firm's public design language

Looking across the projects, a very consistent design vocabulary emerges.

### 1. Courtyard-based planning
California Layout, Neeranjanam and Abhyudaya all use courts/voids as major spatial devices.

### 2. Strong indoor–outdoor connection
Landscape, external views, water bodies and gardens repeatedly become part of the architecture rather than decoration.

### 3. Material-led architecture
Their projects frequently feature combinations such as:

**wood + stone + concrete + metal**

rather than heavily ornamental finishes.

### 4. Controlled natural light
Skylights, courtyards, double-height spaces, jaalis and elongated openings are recurring devices.

### 5. Indian cultural references
More recent projects increasingly integrate:

- Kerala traditions
- South Indian architectural concepts
- Vastu
- pooja spaces
- ritual circulation
- courtyards.

### 6. Modern interpretation
They do not appear to be practicing purely traditional architecture; their work takes traditional planning ideas and packages them within contemporary residential forms.

This evolution is particularly obvious when comparing **California Layout → N170 → Neeranjanam → Abhyudaya**. [India's Best Homes](https://www.indiasbesthomes.com/post/house-at-california-layout-by-architecture-swath-yelahanka-bangalore?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
