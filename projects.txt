# 2. House at California Layout

This is clearly the studio's **breakthrough and best-documented project**.

### Basic project data

**Project:** House at California Layout  
**Location:** Yelahanka, Bengaluru, Karnataka  
**Typology:** Private residence  
**Client:** Mr. Sidhu & Mrs. Radha  
**Family:** Four  
**Plot:** 5,000 sq ft  
**Plot dimensions:** 50' × 100'  
**Built-up:** 10,000 sq ft  
**Floors:** Four storeys  
**Rooms:** 4  
**Car parks:** 2  
**Orientation:** North-facing  
**Vastu:** Yes  
**Design year:** 2019 according to Amazing Architecture  
**Completion:** 2021 according to ArchiDiaries/Amazing Architecture; Buildofy gives 2021–2022. [ArchiDiaries](https://archidiaries.com/projects/house-at-california-layout-architectureswath/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The project document published online identifies **Meinathan N and Sai Harini Karthikeyan** as principal architects and **Venkatesh K and Joshin Rose** as the project team. [Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/776910948/House-at-California-Layout?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### The client's brief

The house was intended to be:

**simple but bold**

The clients wanted contemporary architecture without making the home cold. They also wanted a degree of luxury, while retaining warmth and a connection with nature. [ArchiDiaries](https://archidiaries.com/projects/house-at-california-layout-architectureswath/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### The central design idea

The architects describe the **floor plan itself as the master of the project**.

Rather than treating landscaping as something added around the building, they used it as part of the spatial organization. An **L-shaped landscape occupies the north-eastern side**, deliberately making use of wind and evening shade. [ArchiDiaries](https://archidiaries.com/projects/house-at-california-layout-architectureswath/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The strongest architectural device is the **central water body on the first floor**, which becomes both:

- a visual focal point
- a spatial connection between levels
- a device connecting the interior with greenery. [ArchiDiaries](https://archidiaries.com/projects/house-at-california-layout-architectureswath/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### Ground floor

The ground floor deliberately separates more private/quiet family functions from the main social areas.

The project material identifies:

- game room
- home theatre
- office cabin
- library

on this level. [Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/776910948/House-at-California-Layout?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

This is significant because the studio wasn't simply stacking bedrooms and living rooms floor by floor; they were organizing the house according to **privacy and activity levels**.

### First floor

The principal social zone begins here.

The entrance leads into a passage with a **detailed teakwood ceiling**.

The design deliberately minimizes hard partitions and instead uses:

- level changes
- visual continuity
- double-height spaces
- courts
- water
- material transitions

to differentiate spaces. [Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/776910948/House-at-California-Layout?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The **formal living room is double-height** and slightly sunken. It visually merges with the informal living area, creating the perception of a larger continuous court. [Volume Zero](https://volzero.com/volzero/public/articles/view/house-at-california-layout-by-architecture-swath?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The same floor contains:

- kitchen
- dining
- semi-private living
- central water court. [ArchiDiaries](https://archidiaries.com/projects/house-at-california-layout-architectureswath/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### Upper floor

The bedrooms are arranged around the central court.

The published description says the bedrooms occupy the eastern and western sides, with balconies looking either toward the road or the private eastern lawn. [ArchiDiaries](https://archidiaries.com/projects/house-at-california-layout-architectureswath/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The next upper level becomes an **entertainment floor**, reflecting the client's preference for leisure and relaxation space after work. [India's Best Homes](https://www.indiasbesthomes.com/post/house-at-california-layout-by-architecture-swath-yelahanka-bangalore?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### Material language

The main materials are:

**concrete + teak/wood + marble**

Grey and white tones form the neutral architectural base, while wood supplies warmth.

Teak appears extensively from the foyer to ceilings and passage details. [ArchiDiaries](https://archidiaries.com/projects/house-at-california-layout-architectureswath/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The project documentation additionally shows **brass accents**, beige marble, WPC and detailed granite elements in terrace-related construction drawings. [Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/776910948/House-at-California-Layout?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### Cost

The project eBook available online states:

**₹5,000/sq ft**

as project cost. [Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/776910948/House-at-California-Layout?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

Houzz currently places the project in a broad **₹5 crore–₹10 crore** project-cost band, but that is a directory range and should not be treated as the studio's certified final construction cost. [Houzz](https://www.houzz.in/hznb/projects/house-at-california-layout-pj-vj~6903963)

The core idea is a **north-to-south axis derived from Kerala Vastu**.

The house is organized into:
